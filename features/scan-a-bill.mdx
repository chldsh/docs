---

The **Scan Bill** tab reads a photo of a bill with AI. myBill.Pal logs the bill as an expense and explains it to you in text and audio.

## Scan a bill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the bill">
    On the **Scan Bill** tab, click the upload area and choose your bill. The upload area accepts PNG, JPG or PDF files. A preview of the bill appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan it">
    Click **Scan Bill & Log Expense**. The button shows **Scanning...** while the AI reads the bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the analysis">
    The **Bill Analysis** card shows what the AI found.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To choose a different bill before you scan, hover over the preview and click the close icon.

## What the analysis shows

| Section | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Vendor** | The name of the store or company on the bill. |
| **Amount** | The total amount of the bill. |
| **Category** | An expense category the AI suggests, such as Groceries, Utilities or Dining. |
| **Spoken Explanation** | A one- or two-sentence summary of the bill, with an audio player to listen to it. |
| **Budgeting Tips** | Suggestions for saving money or budgeting better on this kind of expense. |

## How the bill is logged

After a successful scan, myBill.Pal adds the bill to your [Expenses](/features/expenses) with the suggested category, the total amount and today's date.

If the suggested category isn't one of your budget categories yet, myBill.Pal adds it on the [Budgets](/features/budgets) tab with a budget of `$0.00`.

If the AI can't read the bill, myBill.Pal shows a **Scanning Error** message and logs nothing. Try another image of the bill.
