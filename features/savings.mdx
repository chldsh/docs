---
title: "Savings"
description: "Record savings deposits in myBill.Pal, add 5% monthly interest and review your savings history."
---

The **Savings** tab shows your savings balance, lets you add money to it and lists every deposit and interest payment.

Your dashboard starts with a sample deposit of `$1,000.00`.

## Make a deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    In the **Make a Deposit** card, enter the amount in **Amount to Deposit**. The amount must be greater than zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    Click **Deposit**. myBill.Pal adds the amount to your **Savings Balance** with today's date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add monthly interest

In the **Savings Balance** card, click **Calculate & Add 5% Monthly Interest**. myBill.Pal adds 5% of your current balance as an interest entry. For example, a balance of `$1,000.00` earns `$50.00`.

The button is unavailable while your balance is zero. Each click adds another 5% of the new balance.

## Savings history

The **Savings History** table lists each deposit and interest entry with its date, type and amount, newest first. Your savings balance also appears in the **Total Savings** card on the [Dashboard](/features/dashboard).
