---
title: "Expenses"
description: "Log expenses against your budget categories in myBill.Pal, delete them and export them as a CSV file."
---

The **Expenses** tab is a record of your transactions. The table shows the date, category and amount of each expense, with the newest expense first.

## Log an expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Expenses** tab, click **Log Expense**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Select a category from **Category**. The list shows the categories on your [Budgets](/features/budgets) tab. To use a new category, add it there first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Enter the amount in **Amount**. The amount must be greater than zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date">
    Click **Pick a date** and choose the date of the expense. You can't choose a date in the future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Log Expense**. The expense appears in the table and counts toward the totals on the [Dashboard](/features/dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also log an expense by [scanning a bill](/features/scan-a-bill).

## Delete an expense

Click the trash icon in the **Actions** column of the expense. myBill.Pal deletes the expense immediately, without asking you to confirm.

## Export your expenses

Click **Export Data** at the top of the dashboard. myBill.Pal downloads your expenses as a CSV file named `mybillpal_expenses.csv`. The file has three columns: **Date**, **Category** and **Amount**.

The export includes only expenses. It doesn't include budgets, savings or journal entries.
