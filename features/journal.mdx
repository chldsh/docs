---
title: "Journal"
description: "Write, read and delete notes, thoughts and financial goals in your myBill.Pal journal."
---

The **Journal** tab is a place for notes, thoughts and goals about your finances.

## Add an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a title">
    In the **New Journal Entry** card, enter a **Title**. The title must be at least 3 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the entry">
    Enter your note in **Content**. The content must be at least 10 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Entry**. myBill.Pal adds the entry to **Your Journal** with today's date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read an entry

**Your Journal** lists your entries with their titles and dates, newest first. Click an entry's title to read it. To return to the list, click **Back to list**.

## Delete an entry

Click the trash icon next to the entry, then click **Delete** to confirm. You can't undo this.
