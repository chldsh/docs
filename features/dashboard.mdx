title: "Dashboard"
description: "See your total budget, spending, remaining amount and savings, and compare spending against your budget by category."
---

The **Dashboard** tab is the first thing you see after you sign in. It summarizes your budgets, expenses and savings.

## Summary cards

Four cards at the top of the tab show your totals.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Budget** | The sum of the monthly budgets for all your categories. |
| **Total Spent** | The sum of all your logged expenses. |
| **Remaining** | Your total budget minus your total spending. The amount turns red when you have spent more than your budget. |
| **Total Savings** | Your current savings balance, including deposits and interest. |

## Budget vs. Actual

The **Budget vs. Actual** card lists each budget category with a progress bar. Next to each category, myBill.Pal shows the amount you have spent and the budget for that category, for example `$195.80 / $500.00`.

## Spending by Category

The **Spending by Category** pie chart breaks down your spending by budget category. Only categories with at least one expense appear in the chart. Hover over a slice to see its amount.

## Billy, your financial pal

Billy appears in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard. Billy tells you how much you have spent so far and how much remains in your budget. To hide Billy, click the close icon on the card.
