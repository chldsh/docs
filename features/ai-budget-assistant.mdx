---
title: "AI budget assistant"
description: "Get AI suggestions for an optimized budget for each category, based on your logged expenses and your own budgeting rules."
---

The **AI Assistant** tab suggests an optimized budget for each spending category. The AI budget assistant bases its suggestions on the expenses you have logged and on any budgeting rules you give it.

## Prerequisites

Log some expenses on the [Expenses](/features/expenses) tab first. The assistant reads the category and amount of each expense.

## Get suggestions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your rules (optional)">
    In **Advanced Budgeting Rules (Optional)**, describe any goals or limits you want the AI to follow. For example, `Prioritize saving 15% of total budget.` or `Reduce entertainment spending by 20%.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for suggestions">
    Click **Get Suggestions**. The button shows **Generating...** while the AI works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the recommendations">
    The **AI Recommendations** card appears with two sections:

    - **Optimized Budget Plan**: a table with a suggested budget for each category.
    - **Summary & Tips**: an explanation of the changes, insights and tips for your planning and savings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The assistant doesn't change your budgets. To use a suggested amount, add or set the category on the [Budgets](/features/budgets) tab yourself.

If the assistant can't produce suggestions, myBill.Pal shows an **AI Error** message. Click **Get Suggestions** to try again.
