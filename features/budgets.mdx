---
title: "Budgets"
description: "Add spending categories to myBill.Pal and set a monthly budget for each one."
---

The **Budgets** tab lists your spending categories and the monthly budget for each one. Your categories also appear in the **Category** list when you [log an expense](/features/expenses).

Your dashboard starts with four sample categories: Groceries, Transport, Entertainment and Utilities.

## Add a budget category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Budgets** tab, click **Add Budget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the category">
    Enter a name in **Category Name**, for example `Shopping`. The name must be at least 2 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Enter the monthly budget in **Monthly Budget Amount**. The amount must be greater than zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Budget**. The new category appears in the table and in the **Budget vs. Actual** card on the [Dashboard](/features/dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you [scan a bill](/features/scan-a-bill) whose category you don't have yet, myBill.Pal adds that category with a budget of `$0.00`.
</Note>
