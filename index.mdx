myBill.Pal is your friendly financial assistant. It brings your monthly budget, your spending and your savings together on one dashboard.

myBill.Pal is for anyone who manages a monthly budget and wants a clear view of where their money goes. You can:

- Set a monthly budget for each spending category.
- Log expenses and compare them against your budget.
- Track savings deposits and interest.
- Keep a journal of financial goals and notes.
- Ask the AI budget assistant for an optimized budget.
- Scan a photo of a bill to log it as an expense and hear an explanation of it.

<Warning>
  myBill.Pal keeps your data only while the dashboard stays open. Reloading the page or signing in again resets your budgets, expenses, savings and journal to the sample data.
</Warning>
