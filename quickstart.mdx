This guide takes you from the sign-in page to your myBill.Pal dashboard.

## Prerequisites

You need a web browser and the address of your myBill.Pal app.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open myBill.Pal">
    Open myBill.Pal in your browser. The **Welcome to myBill.Pal** sign-in page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your email address in **Email** and your password in **Password**. Both fields are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Click **Enter Service Menu**. myBill.Pal opens your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The **Create Account** link on the sign-in page doesn't open a sign-up form yet. To start using myBill.Pal, sign in with your email address and a password.
</Note>

## Find your way around

The dashboard has seven tabs. On large screens, the same tabs also appear in the sidebar on the left.

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | See totals and charts for your budget, spending and savings. See [Dashboard](/features/dashboard). |
| **Budgets** | Add spending categories and their monthly budgets. See [Budgets](/features/budgets). |
| **Expenses** | Log and delete expenses. See [Expenses](/features/expenses). |
| **Savings** | Make deposits and add interest. See [Savings](/features/savings). |
| **Journal** | Write and read notes. See [Journal](/features/journal). |
| **AI Assistant** | Get an optimized budget from AI. See [AI budget assistant](/features/ai-budget-assistant). |
| **Scan Bill** | Upload a bill and log it as an expense. See [Scan a bill](/features/scan-a-bill). |

Your dashboard starts with sample budgets, expenses, a savings deposit and journal entries, so you can explore each tab right away.

<Warning>
  myBill.Pal keeps your data only while the dashboard stays open. Reloading the page or signing in again resets everything to the sample data. To keep a copy of your expenses, [export them](/features/expenses#export-your-expenses) before you leave.
</Warning>
